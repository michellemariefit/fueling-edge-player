This week

Four things a day. Two tick themselves, two are yours.

  • Recovery snack ticks itself when you tap "She ate it" on the Recovery window page.
  • Water goal ticks itself when the last jug is tapped on the Hydration page.
  • Recovery dinner and Bed by 10 are yours. Tap the day when they happen.

Tap any box to change it. Three of four in a day keeps the streak.

Why these four

  • Recovery snack: fast-digesting protein + simple carbs within 30 minutes of finishing.
  • Recovery dinner: regular protein + complex carbs + veggies within two hours of finishing.
  • Water goal: her number, in jugs.
  • Bed by 10: the repair hormone runs in the first hours of sleep.

A kid who eats the snack, eats the dinner, sleeps, and eats breakfast is a different athlete than the kid who skipped two of those four, even if they did the exact same practice.
