The Fuel List

Every food, sorted by when it plays.

How to use it. Every food sits in a category, and every category has a time it plays. Tap the foods your kid actually eats. From then on, Game day, Practice day and Recovery show her foods instead of the generic list.

Simple carbs are the fast fuel for right around games. Complex carbs are the slow fuel for meals. Fast-digesting protein works close to practices and games; regular protein belongs at meals and recovery. That's the whole system.
