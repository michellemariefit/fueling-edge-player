The Picky-Eater Playbook

Every recommendation in this program has a real-kid version.

Picky is normal. Foods in the same category do the same job. A kid who eats freeze-dried strawberries instead of a banana is getting the same simple-carb fuel. You never need the specific food. You need a food from the right category, at the right time. When she rejects a food, swap inside the category, never fight at the table.

The two rules that protect all of this

Never force, never label. No food is "good" or "bad," and no meal is a battle. Foods are just fuel that plays at different times. The moment food becomes a fight or a moral issue, you lose the season-long game that actually matters.

Test everything at practice first. New swaps get tried on practice days, never game days.

Make it hers. Take her to the store with the Fuel List and let her pick her own foods from each category. A kid who chose her own game-day snack eats her game-day snack. And use the identity, not a lecture: she's an athlete, and athletes fuel.
